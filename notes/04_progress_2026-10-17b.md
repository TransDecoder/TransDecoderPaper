## Benchmark v2 (`benchmark_v2/`)
See its README.

**Scale**
- 8 organisms.
- Input variants:
  - {complete, fragments} × {stranded, unstranded} × {±random negatives}
  - complete_fullutr (untrimmed UTRs, ± unstranded)
- Tools: pytd, pytd_norefine, pytd_homology, perl_td (fixed), perl_td_v600, perl_td_v571, prodigal, codan, plus two LongOrfs baselines.

**Infrastructure notes**
- The CodAn env needed a libnsl.so.1 symlink.
- run_tool.sh uses `${a[@]+...}` for arrays that may be empty (bash 4.2 compatibility).
- Compute policy: UGER is billed, so all runs from here on are local (`run_all.sh`, `xargs -P`). The first round of benchmark and start-grid jobs ran on UGER.

**Early observations (partial results)**
- Stop-level F1 on complete transcripts: TransDecoder 0.96–0.985 vs Prodigal 0.92–0.94. Prodigal over-predicts (precision 0.86–0.91).
- Fragments: TD stop sensitivity is 0.67–0.80 overall. Almost all misses are fragments whose remaining CDS is under 300 nt (the 100-codon minimum). Above 300 nt it is 95–100%. Report length-stratified; Prodigal's minimum is 90 nt.
- The start-refinement fix lowers exact-start accuracy on 250-nt-UTR complete transcripts (human 14,204 → 13,099; athal 22,965 → 22,381) but raises it on fragments (athal 16,233 → 16,721).
  - The old bug made refinement more permissive, which suits complete transcripts and hurts truncated ones. See next section.

## Start-refinement study (`analyses/start_refinement_stability/`)
`start_strategy_experiment.py` re-runs only the start-refinement step on a `pytd_norefine` run and scores exact starts against truth. With legacy seed 1 it reproduces PyTD exactly (validated).

**Strategies compared**
- legacy: seeds 1–5.
- kfold: deterministic noise filter, 5-fold CV over all features, Mann–Whitney AUC, out-of-fold threshold.
  - Threshold objectives: f1_balanced, f1_raw, youden.
  - Candidate rule: best (current) or first.
- fixed: the full 20/10 window.

**Findings so far (athal, creinhardtii)**
- Legacy is highly seed-dependent: creinhardtii complete exact ranges 10,521–11,674 across seeds 1–5. Windows like (2,3), (19,2) or (4,1) are chosen from near-tied AUCs.
- kfold always selects about (19–20, 9–10). It is stable across fold seeds (±~50 exact starts) and gives the best complete-transcript accuracy:

  | Dataset | none | legacy | kfold f1_balanced |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | athal complete | 21,934 | 22,384–22,815 | 23,444–23,530 |
  | creinhardtii complete | 10,324 | 10,521–11,674 | about 12,170 |

- The threshold objective sets the complete-vs-truncated trade-off. On fragments (half are 5′-truncated, where any move is wrong):

  | Dataset | none | kfold f1_balanced | kfold f1_raw |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | athal fragments | 17,055 | about 15,250 | about 16,850 |

- The `first` rule (most upstream passing ATG) helps athal by about 170 and slightly hurts creinhardtii.
- A coding gate (move only if the skipped segment scores ≤ 0 under the Markov model) was tested.
  - It removes most truncated-ORF errors.
  - In athal it also blocks 76% of correct moves, because athal 5′UTR segments often score > 0. In creinhardtii only 16% are blocked.
  - Not adopted; recorded as a negative result.
