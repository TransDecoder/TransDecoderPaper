### Start-codon choice on release code only (E41)
Brian's idea was to score every in-frame ATG with the release's own Kozak model.
- A later ATG outscores a true first-ATG start in 10.5-20.9% of CDSs (within 50 codons). Only 1.0-4.9% of
  annotated starts are later ATGs; *C. reinhardtii* has 19.5%.
- A "move to the better-context later ATG" rule loses in seven organisms at every setting, and gains at most
  +83 of 17,621 in *C. reinhardtii*.
- These results replaced the development PWM-choice comparison in Methods and in Table S6 (new panel b).
- Brian also asked to drop the "PWM, scale corrected" column, its Results sentence (0.728) and the Methods
  clause about the v6.0.0 threshold scale.
